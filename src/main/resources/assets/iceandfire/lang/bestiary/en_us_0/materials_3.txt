Dragon breath can be captured by
holding an empty glass bottle in
either hand when a dragon's breath
or charged blast strikes you. The
captured breath can be thrown or
fired from a dispenser.



Fire dragon breath burns its victim;
its direct dragonfire damage ignores
Fire Protection and Fire Resistance.
Ice dragon breath freezes its victim.
With Lycanites Mobs installed,
lightning dragon breath also inflicts
Paralysis.
