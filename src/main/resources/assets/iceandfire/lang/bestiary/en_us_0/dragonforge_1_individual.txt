Once you have acquired a
dragon forge core, 3 dragon
forge vents, a dragon forge
input, 8 dragon bone blocks,
and 14 dreadstone bricks,
you can construct the
dragon forge itself.

Every forge is made for one
element. Its core, vents and
input must all be of the same
kind: fire parts are crafted
with fire dragon scale blocks
and a fire dragon heart, and
likewise for ice and lightning.





In order to power the forge,
the breath of a dragon of the
forge's own element is required.
Any other breath will not light it.

Place a tame dragon of that kind
directly in front of the input.
The dragon will begin to breathe
into the input as long as there
are valid items in the forge.
