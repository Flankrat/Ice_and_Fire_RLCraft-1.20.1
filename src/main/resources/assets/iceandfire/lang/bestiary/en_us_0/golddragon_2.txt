A mature Gold Dragon possesses a
very unusual breath. It is neither
ordinary dragon flame, frost, nor
lightning, but a stream of magical
energy resembling raw enchantment.

It does not scorch, freeze or
shatter the land. It harms only
the creatures caught in it.

This breath can still power a
dragon forge, though not in the
way the other elements do. Under
its glow the forge takes on a
golden sheen, and the works of
fire, ice and lightning will not
answer to it at all.



What a golden forge may produce
must be made for gold alone. Old
notes hint that the scales of a
Gold Dragon, steeped in its own
blood within such a forge, become
something remarkable. I have yet
to see it done.

Whatever the true origin of this
golden bloodline may be, its
affinity for knowledge and
enchantment makes it one of the
strangest dragon varieties I have
ever documented.
